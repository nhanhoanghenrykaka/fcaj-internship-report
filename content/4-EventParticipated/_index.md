During FCAJ, I joined two community events that gave me a more practical perspective than I could get from documentation alone. The first event broadened my view of system design, DevOps, data work, AWS community, and working in multinational companies. The second event showed me how teams turn an idea into a product that can be demonstrated and explained under hackathon pressure.

I treated both events as part of my project learning. After each one, I went back to Shopsflow and asked what I could improve in the architecture, troubleshooting approach, or the way I presented the solution.

### [Event 1 - Meet 13/6](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Date:** 13/06/2026  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Main topics:** scalable AWS architecture, AWS career journey, DevOps fundamentals, Data Analytics, and MNC culture.  
&emsp;**Materials:** [Google Drive - Meet 13/06/2026](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1XYe3c3jX0F432hyQiCZBOGF2dDlIEwB4)

The main takeaway for me was that cloud skills do not stand alone. Linux, networking, programming, Git, CI/CD, communication, and problem solving are still the foundations underneath AWS services.

&emsp;**Date:** 25/07/2026  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Main topics:** Agentic AI products, architecture, cost, hackathon experience, MVP scope, demo, and pitching.  
&emsp;**Materials:** [Google Drive - FCAJ x AABW](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1goIcF8jRIGZczB4DBHGTsS6mp41FWmLL)
